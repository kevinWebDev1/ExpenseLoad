Frequently Asked Questions

"Mahine ke end mein paise kahan gaye? Pata hi nahi chalta." How does this app help?

That is the exact reason this app was created! By tracking both your pocket cash and bank accounts in one place, you see your exact live remaining balance after every expense so there are zero surprises at month-end.

How do I track daily milk delivery (Doodh ka hisaab)?

Go to Services and add your milk delivery with your daily rate (e.g. ₹60/Litre). The calendar automatically fills daily. If milk didn't come on any day, tap that date to 'Mark as Absent'. At month-end, tap 'Settle'—the app calculates the exact total and deducts it from your bank wallet automatically.

How does the Udhaar / Friend Lending ledger work?

Add a contact for your friend or roommate. When you lend money, tap Lending and select their name. When they pay back, tap Returned. Tap the Share button anytime to send them a clean WhatsApp text receipt, PDF statement, or Excel spreadsheet.

Can I track freelance and client work?

Yes! You can log freelance deliverables or billable hours as income, track pending receivable amounts, and record client settlements straight into your chosen wallet.

Does it require my bank account passwords or OTPs?

Never. Unlike other apps that demand net-banking logins or read private SMS inboxes, Expense Tracker is 100% private and manual. It never asks for passwords and has zero ads.

Can I export my hisaab to Excel or PDF?

Yes, 100%! Tap the Bill icon on your dashboard to filter by date, category, or wallet, and download full itemized statements in Excel (.xlsx) spreadsheets or branded PDF tables anytime for your records or taxes.
