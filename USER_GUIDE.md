# Expense Tracker: Quick Start Guide 🚀

> **📱 Google Play Store:** [Download on Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.kevin.expensetracker)  
> **🎬 Official Video Tutorial:** [Watch on YouTube](https://youtu.be/dCxXBPtqQlQ)  

**"Mahine ke end mein paise kahan gaye? Pata hi nahi chalta."** 

Isiliye maine ye app banaya — sirf aapke liye! 
Khatabook aur baaki finance apps mein bahut saari faltu cheezein hoti hain, par yahan aapko milega ekdum clean aur fast experience. Chahe aap working professional ho, student ho, ya aapki apni shop ho, ab har ek paise ka hisaab rakhna hua aasan.

### 🔥 Is App Mein Kya Special Hai?
- **Sab Ek Jagah:** Multiple bank accounts, cash, udhaar — sab ek jagah track karo.
- **Har Transaction Pe Update:** Kharcha karte hi bacha hua balance turant dikhe!
- **Track EVERYTHING:** Dost ko udhaar diya ho, income aayi ho, ya bank transfer kiya ho.
- **Smart Lending Tracker:** Doston ke udhaar par Due Date notifications aur Extra Interest (expected return) sab track hota hai.
- **Daily Services Tracking:** Milk, newspaper, gym — in sabka daily hisaab rakhna ab automatic ho gaya hai!
- **100% Secure:** In-built App Biometric lock taaki aapka paisa aur data safe rahe.

Agar aapko apne paison ka full control chahiye, toh bas in simple steps ko follow karein:

---

## Step 1: Onboarding (Apne Accounts Setup Karein)
App open karte hi aapko ek Setup Wizard dikhega:
1. **Login:** **'Continue with Google'** par click karke apni Google ID se login karein.
2. **Get Started:** **'Get Started'** button par tap karke setup shuru karein.
3. **Where Does Your Money Live:** Select karein ki aapko kya track karna hai — **"Cash"** ya **"Bank Account"** *(Recommended: Aap dono hi select kar lein taaki pocket cash aur bank balance dono ka accurate hisaab rahe)*.
4. **Current Balance Dalein:** Cash par tap karke apna current balance dalein, aur isi tarah bank select karke uska balance dalein. Neeche aapko *"2 wallets added"* likha hua dikhega (aap chaho toh aur bhi add kar sakte ho). Phir **'Continue'** dabayein.
5. **Review & Add More:** Agli screen par pichle step ke set kiye gaye dono wallets aur unke balances dikhenge. Agar koi aur bank reh gaya ho, toh **'Add Another Wallet'** par tap karke usey bhi add kar lein.
6. **Finish & Go to Dashboard:** **'Finish Setup'** par tap karein aur **'Go to Dashboard'** dabayein — aapka combined Total Balance turant Dashboard par ready ho jayega!
*(Note: Agar future mein koi naya bank ya wallet add karna ho, toh Profile > Manage Wallets se aasaani se kar sakte hain).*

---

## Step 2: Income, Kharcha aur Transfers
Dashboard par **Plus (+)** icon dabane se aapko 7 chips milengi:

- **Expense (Kharcha Record Karein):** 
  Isme aap apna koi bhi kharcha record karte hain. Jaise Zomato se ₹100 ka pizza order kiya:
  1. Amount enter karein (₹100).
  2. Category choose karein (e.g. Food & Dining).
  3. Payment online ki ho toh apna **Bank Account** select karein, ya cash diya ho toh **Cash Wallet** choose karein.
  4. Note likhein (e.g. "Pizza from Zomato") aur Save karein — aapke wallet ka balance turant update ho jayega!
- **Income:** Salary ya pocket money aayi ho (e.g., Salary ₹15,000 in Axis Bank).
- **Transfer:** Ek bank se dusre bank (ya cash se bank) mein paise shift karne ke liye. Apni custom category banane ke liye `Profile > Manage Categories` use karein!

---

## Step 3: Udhaar Ka Hisaab (Lending & Borrowing 💸)
Lending section doston aur family ke len-den (udhaar) ko track karne ke liye hai:

- **Lending (Dost ko Udhaar Dena):**
  Maan lijiye aapne dost ko ₹500 diye:
  1. **Amount:** ₹500 enter karein.
  2. **Select Contact:** Friend ka naam choose karein.  
     *(Agar aap pehli baar use kar rahe hain, toh yahan koi contact nahi dikhega. Iske liye back aakar bottom bar mein 3-lines (Menu) icon par tap karein > **'Contacts'** par jayein > **'Add Contact'** dabakar friend ka naam save karein. Ab wapas Lending chip mein aayenge toh unka naam dropdown mein dikh jayega!)*
  3. **Wallet Selection:** Select karein paise kahan se diye (Cash ya Bank).
  4. **Note / Description:** Reason add kar lein (e.g. "Train ticket").
  5. **Due Date & Expected Return (Smart Features):**
     - **Due Date:** Reminder ke liye date set karein taaki repayment time aane par app notification bhej sake.
     - **Expected Return:** Agar extra fees ya interest milna hai (e.g. ₹500 diye aur wapas ₹600 milenge), toh wo amount set karein!
- **Returned:** Jab dost paise wapas kare, 'Returned' chip select karein. Pura hisaab clear hote hi 'Settled ₹0' dikh jayega.
- **Borrowed & Settled:** Agar aapne kisi se paise udhaar liye hain toh 'Borrowed' chip use karein, aur jab aap unhe wapas chukayein toh 'Settled' chip use karein.

---

## Step 4: Daily Services (Doodh Wala, Newspaper) 🥛
Ye is app ka sabse powerful feature hai:
1. **'Daily Tracker'** mein naya service add karein (Jaise: "Milk", ₹60 per litre).
2. Calendar automatically fill hota rahega! Kisi din doodh nahi aaya? Us date ko tap karke 'Mark as Absent' karein. 
3. Mahine ke end mein **'Settle'** dabayein. App khud hisaab lagayega, apke bank se paise katega, aur automatic expense record kar dega!

---

## Step 5: Advanced Features & Export 📊
- **Statement & Filters:** Dashboard par 'Bill' icon dabayein. Yahan aap date range, custom month, type, aur wallet ke hisaab se apna pura statement filter kar sakte hain.
- **Biometric Lock:** Profile mein jaakar App Lock on karein, taki aapka financial data private rahe.
- **Export to Excel:** Apna poora hisaab Excel file mein download karne ke liye Profile > Export to Excel dabayein!
