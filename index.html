Common Budgeting App Frustrations, Solved.

Why do bank-syncing apps always break?

A major complaint with finance apps is broken Plaid connections and locked bank accounts. ExpenseTracker relies on fast, offline-first manual entry, giving you 100% control and reliability without ever handing over your bank passwords.

Is my financial data sold to advertisers?

Unlike mainstream free budgeting apps that analyze your spending to sell you credit cards, ExpenseTracker is privacy-first. Your data is stored locally on your device and privately synced to your own cloud backup. We never sell your data.

How can I track money I lent to a friend?

Traditional expense trackers completely ignore peer-to-peer lending (Hisaab). We built a dedicated Udhaar tracking feature directly into the app so you never forget who owes you money or when they paid you back.

Why is my current budgeting app so slow?

Most finance apps require a constant internet connection to load heavy, bloated dashboards. Our true offline-first architecture means ExpenseTracker opens instantly, letting you log a transaction in 3 seconds flat, even with no signal.

What happens if I lose my phone?

A massive complaint with basic apps is losing years of data or dealing with broken CSV exports. ExpenseTracker solves this by automatically syncing an encrypted backup to your private cloud in the background, ensuring flawless recovery.

Why doesn't my balance match my history?

Many apps suffer from frustrating math glitches and "phantom" entries. ExpenseTracker is engineered with a rock-solid local database that enforces strict mathematical accuracy across all your cash, bank wallets, and Udhaar accounts.
