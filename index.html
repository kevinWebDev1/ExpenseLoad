Frequently Asked Questions

How does the Daily Tracker work for milk, cook, or subscriptions?

Set up recurring services with your unit rate (e.g. ₹65/Litre) and daily quantity. Each day is marked automatically, and you can easily adjust quantities or pause during vacations. At month-end, tap 'Pay & Settle' to record the exact expense directly to your chosen wallet.

Can I bill freelance clients and track hourly work?

Yes! Switch to the Clients tab in the Daily Tracker to log billable hours with deliverable notes. The app automatically calculates your total pending receivables, lets you generate professional PDF invoices, and credits your wallet when you record payment.

Can I share statements directly on WhatsApp?

Yes. Every monthly statement has dedicated 1-tap export buttons for WhatsApp, PDF, and Excel. You can send a clean, formatted text summary on WhatsApp or attach the complete PDF attendance sheet in seconds.

Does ExpenseTracker require my bank account login or passwords?

Never. Most finance apps break constantly because third-party bank connections fail, and they sell your data to loan companies. ExpenseTracker is 100% manual and offline-first—giving you complete privacy, zero broken links, and 3-second instant entry.

How does Udhaar (Personal Lending) work?

When you lend money to a friend or borrow for an expense, add it under Lending. ExpenseTracker tracks each contact with real-time net balances so you always know who owes you money and can settle debts with full transaction histories.

What happens if I lose my phone or switch devices?

Your data is backed up to your own private, encrypted cloud account in the background. When you log in on a new device, your wallets, transactions, services, and lending ledgers restore instantly.

Does the app work without an internet connection?

Yes, 100%. Everything runs locally on your phone with zero loading screens. You can log expenses, track services, and check balances anywhere—even in flight mode or areas with no signal.

Can I export my data to Excel for taxes or spreadsheets?

Yes. You can export complete, itemized statements with date, category, description, wallet attribution, and running balance columns into clean Excel (.xlsx) spreadsheets anytime.
