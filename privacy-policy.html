Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 2026

1. Introduction

At ExpenseTracker, your privacy and data security are our highest priorities. Because financial data is highly sensitive, we have designed our application with an "Offline-First" architecture. This Privacy Policy outlines what data we collect, why we collect it, and how we protect it.

2. Information We Collect

When you use ExpenseTracker, we collect the following types of information:

  • Account Information: When you create an account, we collect your email address and authentication credentials via Google Firebase Authentication.
  • Financial Data: Transactions, wallet balances, categories, and contact lending history (Udhaar).

3. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used strictly for providing the application's core functionality to you. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal financial data with third-party advertisers or data brokers.

4. Offline-First & Cloud Sync Security

ExpenseTracker operates primarily offline. Your transaction data is stored locally on your device in a secure database (Hive). To ensure you never lose your data, we provide a background Cloud Sync feature using Google Cloud Firestore.

  • Your data is synced over secure, encrypted connections (HTTPS/SSL).
  • Firestore Security Rules strictly enforce that your data can only be accessed by your authenticated User ID. Nobody else, including our development team, can view your financial records.

5. Device Permissions

We may request access to your device's storage solely for the purpose of exporting your financial statements to PDF or Excel files upon your direct request.

6. Data Deletion

You have the right to delete your account and all associated data at any time. Upon account deletion, all cloud-synced data linked to your User ID is permanently erased from our Firestore database.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us through our official support channels.
